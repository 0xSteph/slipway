# Run on your iPhone

1. Plug in the iPhone with a cable, unlock it and tap **Trust**.
2. Turn on **Developer Mode**: Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode, then restart.
3. Press **Run** in the status bar (or Ctrl+Alt+R).

Slipway builds, signs and installs the app, then launches it and streams its log into the **Slipway: Device Log** panel.
